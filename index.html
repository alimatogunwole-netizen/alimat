Competitor analysis and growth planFor a growth marketing agency, 3 competitors reviewed
Client name withheld

What I reviewed

  • Three competing agencies: positioning, content mix, platforms, website, social proof and how they present results.

What I found

  • Activity was concentrated on one platform, so reach was limited.
  • The feed leaned on static flyers, with little video or proof of work.
  • The website didn't guide visitors from interest to enquiry.
  • Success was measured by posts and followers, not leads.

What I recommended

  • Clear positioning and services packaged around outcomes.
  • A content engine where one idea becomes a video, carousel, thread, article and email.
  • A different role for each platform: discovery, authority, conversation, conversion.
  • A website built around positioning, proof and one clear call to action.
  • Weekly trend monitoring and a KPI dashboard tied to leads and revenue.

Proposed content mix

  • 30% education and thought leadership
  • 25% short video
  • 20% case studies and proof
  • 15% founder and behind-the-scenes
  • 10% promotion

90-day roadmap

  • Month 1: define the ideal customer, package services, set content guidelines and a trend system.
  • Month 2: launch the content engine with video, teardowns and case studies.
  • Month 3: launch a low-cost audit offer, measure results and optimise.

Weekly rhythm

  • Monday: educate and nurture. Educational post, newsletter, social listening.
  • Tuesday: engage and optimise. Community engagement, ad check, audience insights.
  • Wednesday: authority and partnerships. One useful article, collaboration outreach.
  • Thursday: visibility. Join conversations, PR outreach, community.
  • Friday: reach and review. Creator outreach and a weekly performance review.

Weekly targets

  • 10 to 15 meaningful interactions a day
  • 5 collaboration pitches and 5 creator prospects
  • 2 to 3 PR opportunities researched
  • 1 article and 1 newsletter, repurposed into social posts

How it's measured

  • Reach, saves, shares and comments for content
  • Replies and accepted pitches for collaborations
  • Open rate and clicks for email
  • Times adjusted after 2 to 4 weeks of real analytics

Rules I work by

  • Audience first: add value where they already pay attention.

  • Repurpose one strong idea into many formats.
